Transverse Angle (Angular Scale)

The transverse BAO scale measures the angular size of the sound horizon on the sky at a given redshift:

At early redshift (z = 0.510):
θ_BAO(0.51) = 147.09/1981.3 ≈ 0.07424rad ≈ 4.25°

At maximum redshift (z = 2.330):
θ_BAO(2.33) = 147.09/5798.3 ≈0.02537rad ≈1.45°
