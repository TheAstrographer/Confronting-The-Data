The fiducial bandpower binning and multipole ($\ell$) ranges utilized by the prominent cosmic shear and 3×2pt pipelines to segregate cosmological parameters from non-linear astrophysics follow targeted structures:
## 1. KiDS (Kilo-Degree Survey / KiDS-1000)
The [KiDS-1000 analysis](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10117910/1/kids1000method.pdf) utilizes 8 custom-binned bandpowers constructed to balance information content against systematic vulnerability: [1, 2] 

* 
* Total Scope: Spans a range of $76 < \ell < 1500$. [1] 
* Low-Multipole Filter: Bins below $\ell = 100$ are heavily suppressed or explicitly discarded because the large-scale analytical covariance modeling lacks stability at very low multipoles. [3] 
* Linear/Quasi-Linear Domain: The lower bandpowers capture the core linear growth signal, where Intrinsic Alignment (IA) is modeled predominantly via linear NLA (Non-Linear Alignment) extensions.
* Non-Linear Edge: The upper limit is capped strictly at $\ell = 1500$. This explicit boundary shields cosmological parameter estimation (S₈) from extreme, unmodelled baryonic feedback that alters the underlying matter power spectrum at higher frequencies. [1] 
* 

## 2. DES (Dark Energy Survey / Y3 & Y4/Y6 Framework)
The Dark Energy Survey pipelines map cosmic shear and galaxy–galaxy lensing across broad tomographic networks, often evaluating information using discrete $\ell$-band selections. [4] 

* 
* Low-Frequency Splitting: Standard DES harmonic-space analyses employ a sequence of binned intervals:
* Bins with a constant linear width of $\Delta \ell = 30$ spanning $0 \le \ell \le 240$.
   * Logarithmically spaced bins extending up to the mapping ceiling of $\ell \sim 10,244$ with an interval footprint of $\Delta \log_{10}(\ell) = 0.055$. [3] 
* Systematic Isolation & Conservative Cuts: To safeguard cosmic shear data vectors from catastrophic baryonic modeling bias:
* In fiducial map-level analyses, data bins that contain power values exceeding $\ell > 1024$ are entirely excluded.
   * Regions below $\ell < 20$ are dropped to prevent contamination from poorly bounded large-scale window configurations. [3, 5] 
* 

## 3. Core Lensing Bandpower Formula
Mathematically, the pipelines convert the underlying tomographic E-mode power spectrum ($C_{\ell}^{EE}$) into the observed bandpowers $C_{B}^{E}$ by evaluating a specific kernel: [2] 
$$\mathcal{C}_{B}^{E} = \frac{1}{\mathcal{N}_l} \int_{0}^{\infty} d\ell \, \ell \, \mathcal{S}_l(\ell) C_{\ell}^{EE}$$ 

* 
* $\mathcal{S}_l(\ell)$: The bandpower response function designed to capture discrete scales cleanly.
* $\mathcal{N}_l$: The normalization factor constructed so that the resulting bandpower maps onto $\ell^2 C_{\ell}/2\pi$ at the log-center of the designated bin. [2]
